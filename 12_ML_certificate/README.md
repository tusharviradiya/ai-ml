## What AWS expects

AWS describes a target candidate with roughly one year using SageMaker AI, Bedrock, and related AWS services, plus experience in a related engineering or data role. Treat this as the experience level to work toward. Study should include implementation and troubleshooting alongside theory. See the [candidate description in the guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/machine-learning-engineer-associate-02/machine-learning-engineer-associate-02.html).

| Exam domain | Weight of scored content |
|---|---:|
| 1. Data Preparation for ML and AI | 28% |
| 2. ML Model and Foundation Model Development | 24% |
| 3. Deployment and Orchestration of ML and AI Workflows | 24% |
| 4. Operating, Monitoring, and Securing ML and AI Solutions | 24% |

These weights come from the [MLA-C02 guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/machine-learning-engineer-associate-02/machine-learning-engineer-associate-02.html). They apply to domains, not individual services or study modules.

## Exam version and booking details

As checked, the [AWS certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-machine-learning-engineer-associate/) lists **MLA-C02 beta** in English: **85 questions, 170 minutes, USD 75 beta price**. It lists general availability dates as TBD. Check that page and the booking screen again before paying; local taxes and currency can affect the amount.

There are two inconsistencies to know about:

- The guide describes a 65-question structure: 50 scored plus 15 unscored, with a minimum scaled passing score of 720/1,000. The certification page separately gives the beta format above. Use the details for the version you actually book; do not treat 720 as 72% correct.
- The certification page calls the update MLA-C02, but its overview table has an `ME1-C02` code entry. The linked guide consistently says MLA-C02. Verify the exam title and version in the booking screen.

The guide describes single-answer and multiple-answer questions. Multiple-answer items require all correct selections for credit, and there is no guessing penalty. See [exam content](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/machine-learning-engineer-associate-02/machine-learning-engineer-associate-02.html).

## What changed from older study material

MLA-C01-only preparation leaves gaps in Bedrock, foundation models, embeddings, RAG, multimodal data, agents, GenAI evaluation, and AI-specific operations. Compare any course with the [official C01/C02 comparison](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/machine-learning-engineer-associate-02/mla-02-comparison.html).

The checklist and labs are an authored learning plan based on the official outline. Module order, study hours, service priorities, and readiness targets are recommendations, not AWS requirements. AWS says its outline and service lists are non-exhaustive, so no topic list or practice score guarantees a pass.
